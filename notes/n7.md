## Public API

### `bnap-rail-fake`

`FakeRail` (`new(seed)`, `with_ttl`, `set_now`, `set_unavailable`, `pay(hash)
-> preimage`, `pay_amount(hash, received)`, `set_state(hash, PayState)`,
`invoice(hash) -> FakeInvoice`, `invoice_count`, `preimage_for(seed, n)`),
`FakeInvoice` (payment hash, preimage, amount, unit, memo, expires, state,
received, invoice string), `DEFAULT_TTL` (300 s). Preimages are
`H_"bnap-rail-fake/preimage"(seed ‖ n)`, so a test sees the same hashes on
every run. Units `sat` and `msat`. `confirm` reports the payment hash as
canonical bytes, the state and what was **received** (0 until paid).

### `bnap-rail-lnd`

`LndConfig { address, cert, macaroon, expiry, timeout }` (`new`,
`from_env`: `BNAP_LND_ADDRESS`, `BNAP_LND_CERT`, `BNAP_LND_MACAROON`,
`BNAP_LND_INVOICE_EXPIRY`), `LndRail::connect(&config)`,
`install_crypto_provider()`, and the pure helpers `confirmed(hash,
&Invoice)`, `value_msat(amount, unit)`, `status_error(&Status)`. An
`invoice.macaroon` is enough.

### `bnap-rail-phoenixd`

`PhoenixdConfig { url, password, webhook_secret, expiry, timeout }` (`new`,
`from_env`: `PHOENIXD_URL`, `PHOENIXD_PASSWORD` or `PHOENIXD_PASSWORD_FILE`,
`PHOENIXD_WEBHOOK_SECRET` or `PHOENIXD_WEBHOOK_SECRET_FILE`),
`read_secret_file(path, key)` (a `phoenix.conf` or a bare secret),
`PhoenixdRail::new`, `PhoenixdRail::verify_webhook(body, signature) ->
WebhookEvent`, `verify_webhook_signature`, `confirmed(hash,
&IncomingPayment)`, `IncomingPayment`, `WebhookEvent`, `WebhookError`,
`SIGNATURE_HEADER` (`X-Phoenix-Signature`).

### `bnap-rail-nwc`

`NwcRail::new(uri)`, `with(uri, expiry, timeout)`, `from_env`
(`BNAP_NWC_URI` or `BNAP_NWC_URI_FILE`), `confirmed(hash,
&LookupInvoiceResponse)`, `amount_msat`. Calls only `make_invoice` and
`lookup_invoice`.

Every rail: kind `ln`, `PayId` = the 32-byte payment hash, the memo
becomes the invoice's description hash, invoices live `DEFAULT_EXPIRY` =
300 s unless configured (the fake: `DEFAULT_TTL` = 300 s), which must not
exceed the gate's exact-scope offer lifetime (N7-6), `confirm` reports
canonical bytes (the payment hash), `PayState` and the amount in the rail's
unit, and fails
closed (`Unavailable`) on any outage, timeout or answer it cannot read. An
answer about another payment hash is refused.

| Rail | Final when | Amount reported | Refused |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fake | `pay`/`pay_amount` or `set_state(Paid)` | what `pay_amount` received | unknown hash |
| LND | `SETTLED` (not `ACCEPTED`) | `amt_paid_msat`, msat | keysend and AMP invoices |
| phoenixd | `isPaid` | max(`requestedSat`, `receivedSat` + `fees`), msat | 204 (unknown) |
| NWC | `settled` (older wallets: `settled_at` and a preimage that opens the hash) | `amount`, msat | outgoing or untyped transactions, a preimage that does not open the hash |

### `bnap-scheme-l402`

`L402::new(MacaroonKeys, Arc<dyn FundingRail>)` implements
`CredentialScheme` (`SCHEME_ID = "httpauth:L402"`, `NATIVE_KIND =
"l402-macaroon"`, `UNIT = "sat"`). `MacaroonKeys` (`new(secret)`,
`with_retired`, `mint(offer_id, payment_hash, expires)`, `open(&Token) ->
Minted`), `Identifier` (66 bytes: version 0, payment hash, user ID),
`Token` (`new(macaroon_b64, preimage)`, `parse`, `Display` =
`macaroon:preimage`, `Debug` hides the preimage), `Challenge` (`to_header`,
`parse`), `authorization(&Token)`, `credential_from_authorization(value)`,
`HeaderError`, `TokenError`, and `OfferInvoices`.

- `offer`: no params; `Unsupported` for a route-scope or metered offer or
  any unit but sat.
- `native`: one invoice from the rail (memo = `OfferID`), a macaroon with
  caveats `bnap_offer=<OfferID hex>` and `bnap_expires=<offer expires>`, and
  the artifact `l402-macaroon` whose data is the whole challenge
  `L402 version="0", token="…", macaroon="…", invoice="…"`. Refuses
  (`Unavailable`, "the invoice outlives the offer") an invoice whose
  `PayRequest.expires` is later than min(offer `expires`, entry
  `quote_expires`) + 60 s (N7-6).
- `verify`: the canonical token, the macaroon under a held key (root key
  `HMAC-SHA256(secret, identifier)`), its offer and expiry equal to the
  credential's offer, `SHA-256(preimage)` = payment hash. Returns local ID
  = payment hash, rail reference `ln:<payment hash>`, value = the entry's
  amount (a claim; admission counts at most what the rail confirmed).
- `settle`: `Ok(None)`, signs nothing.
- `recover_native`: authenticates the macaroon, returns its `OfferID` and
  the part `(httpauth:L402, token bytes)`; the offer comes from the gate's
  cache.
- Scheme codes: `MALFORMED`, `BAD_MACAROON`, `OFFER_MISMATCH`,
  `BAD_PREIMAGE`, `NOT_PAY`, `UNIT`, `WITNESS`.

`OfferInvoices::new(Arc<dyn FundingRail>)` is a `FundingRail` that mints at
most one payment request per (memo, amount, unit), so L402 and MPP share one
invoice per offer (DECISIONS row 24); bounded (`DEFAULT_CAPACITY` 4096,
oldest first), concurrent requests share the first one's invoice, and a
failed request is retried.

### `bnap-scheme-mpp`

`LightningCharge::new(ChallengeKeys, realm, Network, Arc<dyn FundingRail>)`
implements `CredentialScheme` (`SCHEME_ID = "payment:lightning/charge"`,
`NATIVE_KIND = "mpp-challenge"`, `UNIT = "sat"`, `OFFER_MEMBER =
"bnap_offer"`). `ChallengeKeys` (`new(secret >= 32 bytes)`, `with_retired`,
`bind`, `verify`), `Challenge` (all auth-params; `binding_input`,
`compute_id`, `bound`, `verify_id` in constant time, `to_header`, `parse`),
`ChargeRequest` (`encode`, `decode`), `Credential` (`new`, `encode`,
`decode`), `Receipt` (`encode`), `Network`, `MppError`, `authorization`,
`credential_from_authorization`, modules `json` (strict b64url JCS) and
`time` (`rfc3339`).

- `native`: one invoice (via the shared `OfferInvoices`), `request =
  b64u(JCS({"amount": "<sat>", "currency": "sat", "methodDetails":
  {"invoice", "network", "paymentHash"}}))`, `expires` = RFC 3339 of the
  offer's `expires`, `opaque = b64u(JCS({"bnap_offer": hex(OfferID)}))`,
  `id` = HMAC-SHA256 of `realm|method|intent|request|expires|digest|opaque`
  (the draft's three vectors reproduce). Artifact `mpp-challenge`, data =
  the whole `Payment …` challenge. Refuses (`Unavailable`) an invoice
  that outlives the offer or the entry's quote, as L402 does (N7-6).
- `verify`: the canonical credential token, a challenge this gate issued
  (id under a held secret, realm, `lightning`, `charge`, no `digest`,
  `header` or `description`), `opaque` and `expires` naming this offer,
  amount and network equal to the entry's and the scheme's, no `source`,
  `SHA-256(preimage)` = `paymentHash`. Local ID = payment hash, rail
  reference `ln:<payment hash>`: the same `RailId` as L402.
- `settle`: on OK, `scheme_result` = the `Payment-Receipt` value
  (`{challengeId, method, reference: payment hash, status: "success",
  timestamp}`); on FAILED, nothing. Signs nothing.
- `recover_native`: checks the id, returns the `OfferID` in `opaque`.
- Scheme codes: `MALFORMED`, `UNKNOWN_CHALLENGE`, `OFFER_MISMATCH`,
  `AMOUNT`, `INVALID_PREIMAGE`, `SOURCE`, `NOT_PAY`, `UNIT`, `WITNESS`.

### `e2e`

`Gate::new(rail, schemes)` with `issue(scope, &[(scheme, sat)], nonce, now)`
(exact-scope offer, each scheme's `offer` params and native, signed with a
MAC, cached by `OfferID`), `handle(&Credential, scope, now)` and
`handle_native(scheme, native_bytes, scope, now)`, returning a signed
`Response` or `Refused { code, scheme_code }`; `GateSigner`, `registered`
(class O, single, refund none for both Lightning schemes), `OFFER_LIFETIME`.
The flow: 4.4 checks (`GateOfferCheck::check_credential` or
`check_native`), binding classes, scheme `verify` with a verify-only
`CappedSigner`, `confirm_rail_id` for every rail reference, `process`, the
scheme's settle under `CappedSigner::for_change` with the guard closed after
it, `outcome::settle` (timeout and refund paths handled), receipt from
`Recorded::unsigned_receipt`. Store: `MemoryStore`. It is a harness, not the
gate.

## Decisions

- **The `mpp` crate is not used.** Its core turns on `serde_json/
  preserve_order`, which feature unification would apply to every crate in
  a workspace build (bnap-core's JSON included), adds a second
  `serde_json_canonicalizer` and `time`, and has no `lightning` method. What
  the scheme needs (the HMAC binding, auth-params, three JCS objects) is a
  few hundred lines here. The `mpp` workspace dependency is now unused
  (cargo-deny warns; remove it or leave it for a later method).
- **One native entry per Lightning pay entry, holding the whole challenge.**
  The data is the exact `WWW-Authenticate` value, invoice included, so a
  client compares the challenge it received with the signed offer byte for
  byte (9.3). No separate `bolt11` entry is made: the invoice is inside both
  challenges and its description hash is the `OfferID`.
- **One invoice per offer, shared** through `OfferInvoices`, which the gate
  wraps around its Lightning rail once and gives to both schemes.
- **Stateless binding.** L402: the macaroon's root key derives from an
  L402-only secret and the identifier. MPP: the HMAC id of
  draft-httpauth-payment-01 5.1.2.1.1 with the `OfferID` in `opaque`, so
  the lightning draft's "store the challenge" step (9, 9.1) is replaced by
  the binding, and consume-once by the socket's commit of the UseId and the
  shared `RailId`.
- **Canonical bodies (5.1) are enforced strictly.** L402: standard base64
  with padding of the V2 binary macaroon, written back byte for byte, no
  location, exactly the two caveats, one `:`, 64 lower-case hex digits.
  MPP: unpadded base64url, I-JSON (`bnap_core::manifest::parse_strict`),
  bytes equal to their JCS form, only known members.
- **Schemes check no clock at verify.** The macaroon's `bnap_expires` and
  the MPP `expires` must equal the offer's `expires`; whether the offer is
  still live is the gate's 4.4 check, so a recorded outcome can still be
  replayed after expiry (N3b-22).
- **An invoice never outlives its offer (repair round 1).** At `native`
  both schemes compare the rail's invoice expiry with the offer's: the gate
  takes a credential until min(`expires`, `quote_expires`) + 60 s (4.4
  step 4), so an invoice payable later would take a payment the gate then
  refuses STALE, and class O with refund `none` keeps it. A native-only
  client reads only the invoice's expiry (not `bnap_expires`), so it cannot
  avoid this itself. The schemes fail closed (`Unavailable`) instead, the
  rails default to 300 s, and the harness rails are configured to
  `e2e::OFFER_LIFETIME`. With expiry = offer lifetime, a payment made in
  the invoice's last second still has the 60 s skew to be presented.
- **The L402 `user_id`** is `H_"bnap-scheme-l402/user"(OfferID ‖
  payment_hash)`, not random: no client tracking, no state.
- **LND crypto provider.** `fedimint-tonic-lnd` needs a process-wide rustls
  provider; the rail installs aws-lc-rs (the one reqwest's `rustls` feature
  brings) and so does the NWC rail. Feature `tls-aws-lc`; only
  `lightningrpc` is compiled in.
- **phoenixd amount.** A settled BOLT 11 invoice was paid at least its
  amount (the receiver refuses less), while `receivedSat` is net of the
  operator's liquidity fees and truncated to sat; reporting the net value
  would refuse a client who paid exactly (UNDERPAID) and keep its money.

## What downstream must know

**Gate (N9).**
- Wrap the Lightning rail once: `let ln: Arc<dyn FundingRail> =
  Arc::new(OfferInvoices::new(rail.clone()))`, give `ln` to `L402::new` and
  `LightningCharge::new`, and confirm with `confirm_rail_id(&*rail, ..)`.
- Registry facts the gate passes, never the plug's: both schemes class O,
  `UseModel::Single`, `Refund::None`. Exact-scope offers only; never in a
  metered offer (both `offer` methods refuse).
- Cache every offer issued with natives by `OfferID` (5.1): credentials name
  it by `offer_id`, and native recovery looks it up there.
- Native path: `bnap_scheme_l402::credential_from_authorization` (`L402` or
  `LSAT`) or `bnap_scheme_mpp::credential_from_authorization` (`Payment`),
  then `scheme.recover_native`, the cached offer,
  `GateOfferCheck::check_native`, `native_context` and `native_digest` over
  the same bytes, and the rest as for a Credential (`e2e::Gate::handle_native`
  is that flow). Reject a request with two value credentials (5.4).
- Send each native entry's data verbatim as a `WWW-Authenticate` value
  next to the BNAP challenge, `Cache-Control: no-store` on the 402
  (lightning-charge 10). Put MPP's `scheme_result` in `Payment-Receipt`
  next to `BNAP-Receipt` (ERRATA row 33); L402 has no receipt of its own.
- Secrets: one 32-byte L402 secret and one MPP secret of at least 32
  bytes, distinct and used for nothing else; keep retired ones until every
  offer minted under them has expired.
- Keep each rail's invoice expiry at or below the exact-scope offer
  lifetime (N7-6; rail defaults 300 s). A gate with shorter offers, or an
  entry with a shorter `quote_expires`, must lower the rail's expiry: the
  schemes refuse at `native` an invoice that expires after min(`expires`,
  `quote_expires`) + 60 s, so `issue` fails UNAVAILABLE rather than offer
  it. Treat that error at startup as a configuration error. Bound offers
  (each mints an invoice) per client address (12, DECISIONS row 24).
- `phoenixd` webhooks: `PhoenixdRail::verify_webhook(body, header)`; treat
  an event as a hint, still confirm through `confirm_rail_id`.

**Wallet (N10).** L402: `Challenge::parse`, pay the invoice,
`Token::new(&challenge.token, preimage)`, then `authorization(&token)`
natively or the BNAP body `token.to_string()`. MPP: `Challenge::parse`,
`ChargeRequest::decode` (check amount and network), pay,
`Credential::new(challenge, preimage).encode()`, then `authorization` or the
BNAP body. Neither crate decodes BOLT 11: the wallet must decode the invoice
and check its amount, payment hash and description hash (= `OfferID`)
before paying, and compare the challenge with the offer's native entry
(9.3). Never send `source` in an MPP credential to a BNAP gate (N7-1).

**e2e (N15).** `crates/e2e` has the harness and the feature. The regtest
tests run `devenv/regtest/up.sh` themselves (idempotent, serialized with a
lock directory) unless `BNAP_REGTEST_LND_ADDRESS`, `_CERT` and `_MACAROON`
are set; the payer pays with `docker compose exec -T payer lncli
--network=regtest payinvoice --force --json`, retried a few times. `up.sh`
returns only after a 1 sat warm-up payment went through: right after the
channel opens, the payer's router can still see no balance
(`insufficient_balance`). Stop with `devenv/regtest/down.sh`. Memory limits:
bitcoind 256 MiB, each LND 320 MiB.

## Known gaps

- The invoice-expiry bound is checked after the rail minted the invoice
  (`FundingRail::request` takes no expiry): a misconfigured rail leaves an
  unpaid invoice on the node that no offer carries. The rails compute
  `PayRequest.expires` from the gate host's clock; the 60 s skew in the
  bound absorbs a node clock up to 60 s ahead when expiry = offer lifetime.

- No BOLT 11 decoding: the schemes take the payment hash from the rail's
  `PayId` (operator-trusted) and fill MPP's `paymentHash` from it.
- The NWC rail is unit-tested only (no relay or wallet in `devenv`); the
  phoenixd rail is tested against a stand-in, not a real phoenixd.

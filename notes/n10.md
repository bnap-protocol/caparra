# Integration notes: N10, the client side

Branch `node/n10`. Crates `crates/caparra-wallet`, `crates/bnap-cli`
(binary `bnap`) and `crates/bnap-mcp` (binary `bnap-mcp`). Normative
source: `bnap/spec/socket-draft-00.md`, with `spec/schemes/bnap-notes-draft-00.md`,
`spec/DECISIONS.md`, `spec/ERRATA.md` and the registry export (embedded as
`crates/caparra-wallet/data/registry.json`, digest pinned by a test). Built
on the N3 to N8 APIs. **No change to `bnap-core`, `bnap-plug` or any scheme
crate**, except one build line (below).

### `bnap` (crate `bnap-cli`)

`bnap [--wallet PATH] [--task NAME] [--json] <command>`:
`init [--slot N]`, `fund <origin> <sat> [--issuer URL]`,
`get|post <url> [--max-sat N] [-H 'Name: value'] [-d TEXT | --data-file F]`,
`budget [show [--origin O] | set ...]`, `receipts [list | verify [ID]]`,
`restore --origin O... [--issuer URL]`, `resume`,
`paysource [show | nwc [URI] | command ARGV... | none]`.
`budget set` takes `--per-request --per-origin-day --per-task --daily
--allow-scheme --deny-scheme --require prop=value --unrequire prop
--linkable never|fallback|allow --linkable-penalty --allow-experimental
true|false --attest --no-attest --max-balance --fee-base --fee-ppm`.
Environment: `BNAP_WALLET` (default `~/.bnap/wallet.db`), `BNAP_PASSPHRASE`
(else a prompt), `BNAP_TASK`, `BNAP_MNEMONIC` (restore), `BNAP_NWC_URI`.
Every refusal prints `error[CODE]: message` plus the cap left, the property,
the scheme code or `retry_after`; `--json` prints `{"ok": false, "error":
{...}}` with the same fields. Exit status 2 for what the caller can fix
(input, policy, budget, wallet), 3 for "try later", 1 otherwise. The
library half (`cli::run`, `render`) is what the tests drive.

### `bnap-mcp`

`bnap-mcp [--wallet PATH] [--task NAME] [--http 127.0.0.1:PORT]
[--passphrase-file F]` serves over stdio, or streamable HTTP at `/mcp` on a
loopback address only (refused otherwise: whoever reaches it spends within
the caps). Three tools, `BnapTools`:

- `fetch_paid { url, method?, body?, max_sat? }`: status, content type,
  body (text or base64, 256 KiB max) and the payment.
- `budget { action: get|set, cap?: per-request|per-task|per-origin|daily,
  sat?, origin? }`: owner caps, session caps, caps in force, what is left,
  notes held. `set` only narrows; above the owner's cap is `ABOVE_HARD_CAP`.
- `receipts { action: list|verify, id? }`.

Arguments are `deny_unknown_fields`, so a call naming a scheme is refused.
Refusals are `CallToolResult` errors with structured content `{"error":
Refusal}`, the CLI's codes. No tool returns a key, the seed or a pay source
secret.

## The paid fetch (what `Wallet::fetch` does)

1. `https` only. `max_sat` above the person's per-request cap is refused
   before anything is sent; below it narrows the call.
2. Send without a credential. Not 402: done.
3. Pin (9.3): a live pin is used; otherwise the manifest is fetched over
   HTTPS (at most once per 5 minutes per origin, one fetch at a time per
   wallet) and accepted with `bnap_core::manifest::accept`. A manifest that
   fails keeps the old pin until it lapses.
4. No BNAP offer in the 402: refused with `BNAP_REQUIRED` if pinned with
   `required`, refused with `NO_OFFER` if pinned at all (no native challenge
   is paid unless a signed offer lists it), paid natively (L402 or MPP
   `Authorization`) only for an origin with no pin.
5. Every offer passes `Offer::check_client` against the pin's keys, this
   request's action scope and the manifest route's scope. An unknown offer
   key refetches the manifest once (`UNKNOWN_KEY` after), a bad signature is
   `BAD_OFFER_SIG`. BNAP offers are never paid without a pin.
6. Candidates: each pay entry the policy admits (allowed list, registry
   maturity, required properties, linkability), in a unit the wallet can
   count (`sat`, `msat`). Notes held at the origin (only notes whose face
   value fits every cap), or funding in the 402 when none pay (linkable),
   or the L402 / MPP native challenge of that pay entry, whose invoice must
   be for the entry's amount with description hash = OfferID. Price after
   the attestations the wallet sends (waive, discounts; quotas not counted
   on). Ranked by price plus fees, linkable ones dropped while an
   unlinkable one can pay (or penalised under `allow`), server order breaks
   ties. The first plan whose **risk** fits every cap is taken.
7. Reserve the risk in the ledger, lock the notes and advance the counters
   in one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction, store the operation (request with
   credential), then pay (Lightning) and send. Exactly one value credential
   plus the attestations the policy allows.
8. Receipt: `Response::check_client` against the pin, change through
   `recover_change` (every DLEQ checked), then notes, ledger (trued up to
   the real cost), quote state and the receipt record in one transaction.
   A receipt or change that fails its checks leaves the operation disputed,
   the notes locked and the reservation counted.
9. Errors: PENDING is retried in place (up to 10 s), otherwise kept for
   `bnap resume`; UNAVAILABLE and network losses after sending are kept for
   `resume`; SPENT marks the notes spent; every other code releases the
   notes (and mints a paid quote back); BAD_OFFER, STALE and WRONG_SCOPE
   retry once with the fresh offer when nothing went out over Lightning.

## Decisions

- **Risk, not price, against the caps.** A notes payment reserves the face
  value of the notes it sends (a gate that kept the change keeps them all),
  a funded one the quote plus the routing fee ceiling, a Lightning one the
  invoice plus the fee ceiling. The entry is trued up to the real cost (sent
  minus change, fees included) when the receipt arrives. Notes worth more
  than the caps leave are never offered.
- **Routing fees count, in full.** `FeePolicy` gives each Lightning
  payment a fee ceiling that is reserved with it and passed to the pay
  source; the fee the pay source reports is recorded in full, above the
  ceiling or not. NIP-47 `pay_invoice` carries no fee limit and a
  `Command` pay source applies one only through `{max_fee}`, so with those
  a single payment can overshoot a cap by its fee excess; the next
  reservation sees it.
- **A pay source error is not proof that nothing was paid.** A timeout
  (NWC gives up after 90 s, a hold invoice may settle later) leaves the
  reservation counted against the caps, and the quote of a funding
  payment stays saved; `bnap resume` mints unpaid quotes the issuer says
  were paid.
- **A Lightning payment always leaves proof.** The invoice and preimage
  are stored before the paid request goes out (native) or with the
  operation (BNAP); a refused or lost request keeps them as a native
  receipt (`ok = false`).
- **Caps.** Per request, per task (no window), per origin and daily (24 h
  rolling). A task is one CLI command or one MCP server run unless
  `--task` names a group.
- **No BNAP payment without a pin.** Draft-00 4.4 allows trust on first
  use for offers; the wallet instead refuses BNAP offers from an origin
  whose manifest it could not pin, because a receipt can only be checked
  against a pinned receipt key and the brief asks for receipts verifiable
  later. Native challenges of an origin without any pin are paid (9.3).
- **No refresh without a reason.** A live pin is refetched only for an
  unknown offer key (rate limited); `required` set later reaches the
  client when its pin lapses or a key changes (9.3 has no refresh rule).
- **Experimental opt-in.** Every value scheme in the registry is
  `experimental`, so a new wallet pays nothing until a person runs
  `bnap budget set --allow-experimental true`. `bnap init` says so.
- **Pay source trait.** bnap-plug's `PaySource` takes a `PayRequest` and
  reports no fee; the wallet needs the fee ceiling in and the fee out, so
  it has its own `paysource::PaySource`. bnap-plug is unchanged.
- **Funding in the 402** buys exactly the price (no top-up), so the
  per-request reservation stays small and change is rare; `bnap fund` buys
  unlinkable credit in advance.
- **Mint outputs are the binary split of the quote** (NUT-04 needs outputs
  that add up to it); change outputs are the canonical ladder (6.3).
- **Restore counters** move past everything scanned, so a restored wallet
  never sends a `B'` again. Restore scans the recorded slots, this
  device's, slot 0 and every `--slot` named: a seed restored without its
  backup cannot know other devices' slots.
- **Storage.** argon2id 64 MiB, 3 passes; OS keychain not used (not
  trivial without a platform dependency). The file shows record kinds and
  counts, not origins, notes or keys.
- **Offer by ID** for route-scope offers, inline otherwise (fund quotes
  must be inline, bnap:notes 5.4).
- **No binding** (`tls-exporter` is not reachable through reqwest);
  bnap:notes 15 makes it a SHOULD.
- **One build line outside the three crates**: `bnap-scheme-notes`'s
  self dev-dependency now goes through the workspace entry, as cargo-deny's
  `workspace-duplicate` ban requires once a second crate depends on it.
  Root `Cargo.toml` adds `opt-level = 3` for `argon2` and `blake2` in the
  dev profile (the KDF takes seconds unoptimized).

## Known gaps

- No HTTP carrier crate on develop, so the test server is built from
  bnap-core directly and requests go to it in process (`TestTransport`);
  nothing here opens a socket or runs TLS. The real `HttpsTransport` is
  only exercised by the binaries' start-up tests.
- No metered route, attestation verification or streamed receipt in the
  test server (the wallet sends Web Bot Auth and DPoP bodies and reads
  `bnap-receipt` events, untested end to end).
- A crash between a Lightning payment and storing its preimage (no I/O in
  between, only the credential build) loses that payment.
- `resume` tries to mint every unpaid quote, which takes fresh counter
  values each time (gaps the restore rule covers).
- The NWC pay source is not tested against a wallet (no relay in
  devenv); `CommandSource` is tested on `lncli` output only.
- No swap of notes near their keyset's final expiry (bnap:notes 10).
- No `tls-exporter` binding.

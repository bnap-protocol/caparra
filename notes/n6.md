Branch `node/n6`, crate `crates/bnap-scheme-notes`. Normative source:
`bnap/spec/schemes/bnap-notes-draft-00.md` (2026-10-13, bnap commit
`e90fd8c`), under `spec/socket-draft-00.md`. Built on the `bnap-core` and
`bnap-plug` APIs of `notes/n3-core.md`, with one additive change to
`bnap-plug` (below).

## How a gate (N9) uses it

The flow is the one of `notes/n3-core.md`, "How a gate runs a request",
with `NotesScheme` registered as `bnap:notes`, use model `single`,
`refund = atomic`. `tests/common/mod.rs` (`Gate::handle`) is this list as
running code.

1. **Offers.** `scheme.offer(cx)` returns the route-scope `params`
   (issuer, every offerable keyset, the limits and `max_fund` from
   `NotesConfig`). It returns `Unsupported` (leave notes out of the offer)
   when the offer's admission cutoff is less than 60 s after `cx.now`
   (5.4: an exact-scope notes offer lives at least 7 minutes at the default
   `pending_timeout`). For a request that carried `BNAP-Fund`: parse it
   with `FundRequest::parse_header`; on a route that funds, call
   `create_fund_quote(&req, route_id, &offer_nonce, now)` (`None` means no
   funding here) and build the exact-scope entry with `offer_with_quote`.
   That offer MUST NOT be cached (5.4). Rate-limit quote creation per
   client address, and never make a quote without `BNAP-Fund` (8.2).
2. **Verify.** `CappedSigner::verify_only(signer, "sat")`, then
   `scheme.verify`. Backing is `view.notes()` plus every confirmed rail
   payment. A funded credential returns one `ln` rail reference (the
   quote's payment hash): confirm it with `confirm_rail_id` and map a
   failure with `rail_error` (`QUOTE_UNPAID`, or UNAVAILABLE). On a route
   whose `require` sets `link-payment = unlinkable`, answer POLICY when
   `funded(&verified)` (8.4, P36).
3. **Process.** Give `outcome::process` the fields of
   `admission_fields(offer.fields(), limits.pending_timeout)`, never the
   offer's own. That lowers the notes entry's `quote_expires` to the
   admission cutoff, so a new notes credential after the cutoff is STALE,
   while a replay is answered from its outcome (5.4: the cutoff applies
   after the replay lookup). Pay value: `claimed = verified.value`,
   `refund = Refund::Atomic`.
4. **Settle.** `CappedSigner::for_change(signer, store, charge.cap())`
   around `scheme.settle`. **If the scheme's OK settle fails, settle the
   outcome FAILED instead** (`pending.charge(Failed, 0)`, the scheme's
   FAILED settle under that cap, then `outcome::settle`): when the sweep
   timed the outcome out while the action ran, the OK settle can reserve
   no fresh change and this is how the client gets its refund in the same
   request. A FAILED settlement of a metered offer passes 0 units (P35).
5. **NUT endpoints.** NUT-04 `POST /v1/mint/bolt11` is
   `scheme.mint(&MintEnv { store, signer, limits, now }, &req)`; NUT-23
   quote checks are `quote_state` (report `ISSUED` when `issued`); NUT-09
   restore is `restore`; NUT-01/02 keys come from the issuer table
   (`KeysetInfo::cashu_keys`, `id`, `input_fee_ppk`, `final_expiry`).
   Refuse every NUT-03 swap and NUT-05 melt whose inputs are on a BNAP
   keyset (4.2). Map `MintError` to the NUT-04 errors (`Issued`,
   `OutputSigned`, `Unpaid`, `BadSignature`, ...).
6. **Rotation.** On a schedule: `plan_rotation(&table, now, &policy)`,
   `apply_rotation`, `put_keyset` for each changed entry, and publish a new
   standby keyset (and give its secrets to the signer) whenever
   `needs_standby`. `revoke` a compromised keyset at once.
7. **Stores.** `IssuerStore` must be durable and shared by every gate
   instance (sqlite or postgres, alongside the use store); `MemoryIssuerStore`
   is not. Keep signed-output records until 60 s after the output keyset's
   final expiry, quote records as long as their `RailId` record, and both
   at least 60 s past the deadline of any entry that committed the quote
   (8.6). The `ln` rail's `PayId` MUST be the 32-byte payment hash, and
   `confirm(RailRef { kind: "ln", reference: payment_hash })` returns
   `canonical = rail::ln(hash)`; a rail that prunes settled invoices keeps
   answering from the quote's paid state.
8. **Signer** (`bnap-signer-*`): hold each keyset's `IssuerKeyset`;
   `verify_unblinded` is `IssuerKeyset::verify_note`, `blind_sign` is
   `ChangeLedger::reserve` then `IssuerKeyset::sign` per output
   (`KeysetSigner` in `test_support` is the reference). Refuse revoked
   keysets.

## How a wallet (N10) uses `client`

- Seed: the 64-byte BIP-39 seed. `derive(seed, keyset_id, counter)` gives
  `k_n` (type `0x02`, P27), `K`, `secret = hex(K)`, `Y`, `r` (type `0x01`)
  and `B'`; `None` means skip that counter. `counter(slot, index)` puts the
  device slot in the top 16 bits. Never reuse a counter once its `B'` was
  sent; record the highest counter per keyset and slot in the backup
  (12.2).
- Paying: `select_notes(&notes, |ks| offer_fee_ppk(ks), price,
  max_inputs)`, then `ladder_outputs(seed, active_keyset, value, next)`,
  `build_body(&notes, &outputs, fund_quote_id)` (sorts inputs by `K`),
  put the body in the Credential, compute the Digest with
  `Credential::digest`, then `sign_witness(&body, &ordered_notes,
  fund_key, &digest, &aux)` and set the part's witness.
- Funding: a fresh quote key, `FundRequest { amount, pubkey: quote_pubkey(&k)
  }.to_header()`, then `check_fund_quote(&q, &pubkey, asked, now)` before
  paying (8.3), save quote, key and offer, pay, and redeem in `fund` with
  `fund_sig`; or mint with `mint_request(quote, &outputs, &k)` and
  `unblind_mint`.
- Change: keep each `PendingOutput` until the Response, then
  `recover_change(&outputs, key_lookup(&keys), value, charged,
  scheme_result)`. It verifies every DLEQ before unblinding and refuses a
  bad one (`BadDleq`, operator evidence). `matches_rule == false` means the
  signed indices are not the 7.2 selection: keep the notes, record the
  signed Response as evidence (see N6-1 for the one case an honest gate
  produces it).
- Keys: take keyset IDs, fees and `active` from the offer, keys from the
  issuer's NUT-01, and check `KeysetInfo::id_matches` / `keyset_id` (5.2).

## Decisions made

- **One encoding, every refusal NON_CANONICAL.** The body, witness and
  `scheme_result` parsers refuse framing errors, non-minimal integers,
  unknown records (odd and GREASE included, P2), empty values, missing
  Required records, wrong lengths, invalid points and x-only keys, inputs
  out of order (so no `K` twice) and a repeated `B'`. Builders validate by
  parsing their own output. Parameters follow the even/odd rule (P6).
- **Verification order is 6.2's**, cheapest first: form (`NON_CANONICAL`,
  `EMPTY`, `LIMIT`), keysets (`UNKNOWN_KEYSET`, also for a non-`sat`
  entry or an entry without usable params, as 18.1 says), amounts, fund
  (`QUOTE_UNPAID`), value and ladder, witness, notes through the signer.
  Fees use the offer's `input_fee_ppk` (the offer is the quote); the
  keyset ID covers the fee, so the table cannot disagree for the same ID.
  Verification reads no use state and writes nothing.
- **Settlement signs exactly `min(value - charged, signer cap)`** with
  the 7.2 selection, one `blind_sign` per keyset, checks every DLEQ proof
  against the keyset key before it goes into `scheme_result`, and records
  each signed output under `H(body)` before returning (7.4). Nothing
  selected: `None`, so the Response has no record 16 (P17). Index 0 is
  written as `00 00` (P32).
- **The refund is a superset of what the store reserved** (N6-1). FAILED
  reads the outcome's reservations through `CappedSigner::reserved`: if
  they all lie in the 7.2 selection for the whole value, that selection is
  signed; otherwise the reserved outputs are re-signed (free in the
  ledger) and the 7.2 greedy over the other outputs adds what is left. The
  store's ledger never refuses it, whatever crashed before.
- **The admission cutoff lives in the fields passed to `process`**
  (`admission_fields`), not in a core hook, so no core change was needed
  for 5.4.
- **Minting goes through `outcome::process`** (N6-3): a price-0 request
  whose Context is `rk_mint`, whose only use is the quote's `RailId`, with
  an atomic value of the quote amount, then the outputs signed under the
  commit's change cap and the signatures recorded as the outcome's
  `scheme_result`. That commits the `RailId` in the socket's own store (a
  credential and a mint of one quote exclude each other, SPENT / Issued),
  makes a retry return the same signatures, and bounds a mint by the
  store's ledger. A mint whose signing timed out is answered by its refund
  path (the same outputs, signed under the recorded cap).
- **Quote IDs are `hex(offer nonce)`**, so a nonce never gets two quotes
  (8.2); asked again with the same key, the same quote comes back, another
  key gets none. The invoice memo is `SHA-256("bnap:notes quote " ‖ id)`.
- **`issued` and `paid` on a quote are caches**: the use store decides
  use. `settle` (OK or FAILED) and `mint` set `issued`; a mint that finds
  the `RailId` spent sets it too.
- **Derivation is written here, not taken from cashu**: cashu's NUT-13
  takes a 32-bit counter and has no type `0x02`; the spec's counter is 64
  bits (P27) and `k_n`, `r` are reduced mod n (cashu fails above n). The
  type-`0x01` output equals cashu's for counters below 2³² (tested).
- **Keyset rotation** is a pure planner over the table: activate the
  oldest standby whose 30-day publication lead passed once every active
  keyset signed for its period (91 days by default), move the old ones to
  inputs-only, report `needs_standby` and `blocked`.

## Change to `bnap-plug` (flag for the core re-gate)

`CappedSigner::reserved(&self) -> Result<Vec<ChangeOutput>, SignerError>`
(commit `7868d7d`, 21 lines in `crates/bnap-plug/src/signer.rs`, one test
in `tests/plugs.rs`): read-only, returns `change_outputs` of the outcome's
record for a change view, nothing for a verify-only view. Why: with the
store-backed ledger (N3b-29) one total per outcome covers the OK change
and every refund. bnap:notes' FAILED selection for the whole value is in
general not a superset of the OK selection for `value - charged` (1,680 on
the canonical ladder: change 1,340 = 1,024 + 256 + 32 + 16 + 8 + 4, refund
1,680 = 1,024 + 512 + 128 + 16), so after a late OK settle reserved the
change, the plain refund needs 640 sat of fresh outputs on top of 1,340
reserved, above the 1,680 value: the ledger refuses it on every replay and
the refund is never recorded. The scheme cannot see the reservations
otherwise (a `SignerError` does not say whether the store reserved before
the signer failed). No existing behaviour changes.

Also changed outside the crate: `deny.toml` gains a license exception for
`minicbor` (Blue Oak 1.0.0, OSI-approved permissive), a required dependency
of `cashu` through `ur`.

## Known gaps

- NUT-07 state checks and an exact NUT-23 `issued` (N6-4) need a
  read-only `used(id)` on `UseStore`.
- No HTTP: the 402 offers, `BNAP-Fund`, the NUT endpoints and their JSON
  are the gate's (N9). cashu's `MintRequest`/`BlindSignature` types are
  what `mint` takes and returns, so the NUT-04 JSON is cashu's.
- No durable `IssuerStore`: a sqlite or postgres one belongs with the
  stores or the gate.
- A keyset revoked between `verify` and `settle` is not re-checked at
  settle; the signer should refuse revoked keysets (`KeysetSigner` does).
- BOLT12 (NUT-25) credit, swaps as a manifest route (the gate's; a
  price-0 offer works, tested), withdrawal (section 16) are not built.
- The registry entry (`registry/schemes.toml`) still has the v0.4
  `rail_refs` and `witness` texts; section 14 of the notes draft gives the
  draft-00 ones.
